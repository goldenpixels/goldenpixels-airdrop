Questions

Who is eligible for the airdrop?

Any wallet holding at least one Golden Pixels Series 1 artwork (#001–#020) at the moment of the snapshot, with a completed registration — including the three community tasks (follow on X, subscribe on YouTube, join the Discord). One registration per holding wallet.

When is the snapshot and the launch?

Both dates will be announced in advance on this page, our main site, X and Discord. No date has been set yet — anyone announcing a date elsewhere is not us.

Which wallet receives my airdrop?

The same Ethereum wallet that holds your Golden Pixels artwork at the snapshot. The token launches on Ethereum — the same chain as the NFTs — so there is no second wallet to set up and nothing to bridge. Your address is public information and safe to share; your seed phrase is not, and we will never ask for it.

What is the token called, and how much will I receive?

The name, ticker and per-holder amount will be revealed at launch on our official channels. We announce only what is real — no numbers are promised before the token exists.

I don't hold a Golden Pixel yet. Can I still join?

Yes — artworks #001 First Light, #002 Golden Spiral and #011 Golden Phoenix are listed at 0.003 ETH on Rarible, and more of the 20 will be listed one by one. Buy only through the official site so you know you are getting the real collection.

Does registering cost anything?

No. Registration is free and always will be. The airdrop itself is free for holders — sending it is on us.

⚠ Stay Safe

This page and goldenpixels.github.io are the only official Golden Pixels websites. We will never DM you asking to connect a wallet, pay a fee, or "verify" anything to receive the airdrop — and we will never ask for a seed phrase or private key. If a page asks for those, it is a scam. When in doubt, check the links on our main site or ask in our Discord.
